# Luminous 3D (house style)

The engine's own look, built from the Tradient V8 film: a deep navy world with a perspective floor
grid, drifting light and dust, glowing glass panels with thin borders, neon 3D icons drawn as light,
Anton titles in capitals with a gold key word, Roboto Condensed labels and subtitle captions, zoom
handoffs between scenes, bloom, vignette and film grain.

Best for: technology, money and markets, "how it works under the hood" explainers.

Do: one glowing hero per line; gold only on the word or number that matters; cyan for light and
movement; teal/pink for up/down; keep panels sparse.

Don't: flat white backgrounds, pastel colours, paragraphs on screen, cartoon shapes.
