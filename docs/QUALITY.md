# Lessons to retain

- A23 reproduction proved replay, not automatic quality for new scenes.
- Generic conveyor belts, swinging gates, ornamental dials, hydraulic presses and receipt printers were rejected for the trading story. Their code remains historical; do not recommend them as the benchmark.
- Scene quality comes from causal transformations, detailed environments, meaningful scale changes and coordinated movement. Repeated panels and camera drift are not equivalent.
- Preserve object identity: one chart, saved configuration, instruction, matched quantities and execution report.
- The phone or full device should establish context briefly; longer passages need a meaningful action or departure into its contents.
- Focus the actual surface the narration discusses. Sample fragments and magnifying optics must share the same texture/data mapping.
- Reveal one concept at a time at readable size. In V8, oversize source panels and a quantity camera that cropped two units were caught in proofs and corrected.
- Freeze the departing editor at its saved state; do not let a later global clock reactivate manual submission.
- On a spoken anchor, the meaning must be readable. Starting a tiny geometry reveal on that word is too late.
- Keep stable environments through matching and report return; execution precedes reporting and quantities remain conserved.
- Changes are opt-in adapters. Old compositions and calibrated native clocks remain intact.
- Store renderer-consumed parameters and describe which prose is author direction rather than executable control.
