# Facts

| # | Fact (as it will be said) | Source (URL or document) | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | iPhones are designed for ambient temperatures between 0 and 35 degrees Celsius (32 to 95 F): "between 0º and 35º C". | Apple Support, "If your iPhone or iPad gets too hot or too cold": https://support.apple.com/118431 | 2026-10-22 |
| F2 | Outside that range in extreme cold, use "might temporarily shorten battery life and could cause your device to turn off"; "Battery life will return to normal when you bring your device back to higher ambient temperatures." | Apple Support: https://support.apple.com/118431 | 2026-10-22 |
| F3 | Cold slows the battery's electrochemical reaction and "increases the internal resistance and lowers the capacity"; the loss "will recover in room temperature". | Battery University BU-502: https://batteryuniversity.com/article/bu-502-discharging-at-high-and-low-temperatures | 2026-10-22 |
| F4 | "Twenty percent" (20%) is an example scenario of a battery reading, not a measured figure. | Illustration | 2026-10-22 |
