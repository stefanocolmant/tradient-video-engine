Your phone says twenty percent. Then it just dies.

It's not broken. It's cold.

Inside the battery, lithium has to move to make power. Cold slows that chemistry down.

So the battery pushes back harder, and the voltage dips. Your phone thinks it's empty, so it shuts off to protect itself.

Apple says iPhones are built for zero to 35 degrees Celsius. Below that, battery life can drop for a while.

The good news? It's temporary. Warm it up, and the charge comes back.
