You tap Buy. A moment later, your app says filled.

In that moment, your order took a trip you never see.

First, it goes to your broker. And your broker usually doesn't send it to the stock exchange.

It sends it to a wholesaler, a giant trading firm. More than ninety percent of everyday investors' market orders go this way.

The wholesaler checks every exchange for the best price. By law, it can't give you a worse one.

Then it trades with you. Done.

But the shares haven't actually changed hands yet. That happens one business day later. It's been the rule since May 2024.

So next time you tap Buy, you'll know where it really went. Follow for more.
